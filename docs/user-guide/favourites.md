# Favourites

## Saving

Click the **heart** on any artwork card or artwork page. A filled heart means it's saved. Click it again to remove it.

## Viewing

Open **Favourites** in the menu. Your saved works appear newest first.

## Privacy and storage

Favourites are kept **only in this browser on this device**. There is no account, and nothing is sent to a server.

- Clearing your browser data removes them.
- In private or incognito windows, they last only until the window closes.

## Moving favourites to another device or browser

1. On the Favourites page, click **Export**. A file named like `atlas-of-art-favourites-2026-11-15.json` downloads.
2. On the other device, open Favourites, click **Import** and choose that file.
3. Imported works are added to any you already have. Duplicates are skipped.

## Clearing

**Clear** removes every favourite from this browser, after asking you to confirm. This can't be undone, so export first if you might want them back.
