# FAQ

**Why do some searches return few results?**
Each filter narrows the search, and museums record places, dates and styles differently. Try widening the era, removing a style, or turning off *Public domain only*.

**Why do results keep arriving as I scroll?**
The site asks several museums at once and shows the first ones to answer. Slower museums' works are added as you scroll.

**A museum shows an orange or red dot. Is something broken?**
That museum is slow or temporarily unavailable. The others still work, and it is retried automatically.

**Why are there fewer works from Africa, Oceania or Latin America?**
Open-access collections are dominated by large museums in North America and Europe. As far as we know, no museum based in Africa offers a free public API yet. African art here comes from the Met, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art, Wikidata and (when enabled) the Smithsonian's National Museum of African Art. Many works from these regions in Western museums have complex histories of acquisition; the museum pages often say more.

**Why does a work have no region or style?**
The site works these out from the museum's description. When the description is vague, the field is left blank rather than guessed.

**Can I download or reuse images?**
Works marked *Public domain / CC0* can be reused freely. For others, check the museum's page through **View at [museum]**.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Favourites are stored in your browser. See [Favourites](favourites.md).

**Is my activity tracked?**
The site sets no tracking cookies. Your searches go to this site's server, which asks the museums on your behalf; nothing personal is sent to them.

**Some images don't load.**
Museums occasionally withdraw or move images. Cards with broken images are hidden automatically.
