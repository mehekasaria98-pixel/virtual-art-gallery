# Exploring by region

The world is divided into nine regions:

| Region | Includes, for example |
|---|---|
| Africa | Egypt, Nigeria, Mali, Congo, Ethiopia, South Africa |
| East Asia | China, Japan, Korea, Mongolia, Tibet |
| South Asia | India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka |
| Southeast Asia | Indonesia, Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, Myanmar |
| West & Central Asia | Iran, Türkiye, Iraq, Syria, Afghanistan, Uzbekistan |
| Europe | France, Italy, the Netherlands, Germany, the UK, Russia |
| North America | United States, Canada |
| Latin America & Caribbean | Mexico, Peru, Brazil, Cuba, Guatemala |
| Oceania | Australia, Aotearoa New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, the Pacific Islands |

## How to choose regions

- **Map (tablet and desktop):** in Explore, open **Region** and click a shape on the world map. Selected regions turn red. Click again to deselect. With a keyboard, Tab to a region and press Enter or Space.
- **Chips (every screen):** click the region names below the map. On a phone, tap **Filters** first.
- **Home page:** click a region on the "Browse by region" map.

You can pick **several regions at once**. Results include works from any of them. Remove a region by clicking the **×** on its pill above the results.

## Good to know

- Regions refer to where a work was **made** (its culture or place of origin), not where the museum is.
- Museums describe places in different ways (for example "Edo period, Japan" or "Yoruba peoples"). The site works out the region from that description, so a few works may show without a region.
- Some regions have fewer works with images in open-access collections. Try widening the era or removing a style if results are thin.
