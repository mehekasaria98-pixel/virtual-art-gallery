# Views, keyword search and sorting

Switch views with the **Grid / Timeline / Map** control above the results. Your filters stay the same in every view.

## Grid

A wall of images in a flowing layout. Scroll down to load more; there is also a **Load more** button at the bottom. Each card shows the title, artist, date and museum.

## Timeline

Results are arranged along a vertical time line:
- grouped by **decade** when your era is a century or shorter
- otherwise grouped by **century**

Each group scrolls sideways. Works without a known date are listed at the end.

## Map

A world map showing **how many of the loaded results** come from each region. Click a region to see its works underneath. Counts grow as you load more results in Grid or Timeline.

## Keyword search

Type in **Keyword, artist, subject…** at the top of Explore, for example *sea*, *Hokusai* or *mask*. The search starts when you pause typing. Keywords combine with all your other filters.

## Sources & sorting

Open **Sources & sorting** in the filters to:
- **Sort** by *Most relevant*, *Oldest first* or *Newest first*. Sorting applies to each batch of results as it loads.
- Switch on **Public domain only** to show only works whose images you may reuse freely (public domain or CC0).
- Tick or untick individual **museums**. Each has a coloured dot showing how it answered:

| Dot | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green, with a number | Answered; the number is how many matches that museum has |
| Orange | Slow; its works will appear as you scroll |
| Yellow | Not used for this search (for example, keyword search on Wikidata) |
| Red | Unavailable right now |
| "key needed" | Not switched on by the site owner |

## Clearing filters

Click the **×** on any pill above the results, or **Clear all**.
