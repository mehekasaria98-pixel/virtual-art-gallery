# Exploring by era

Open **Era** in the Explore filters. There are three ways to pick a time, and you can switch between them freely.

## 1. Periods

Quick presets:

| Period | Years |
|---|---|
| Ancient | 3000 BCE – 499 CE |
| Medieval | 500 – 1399 |
| Renaissance | 1400 – 1599 |
| Early modern | 1600 – 1799 |
| 19th century | 1800 – 1899 |
| Modern | 1900 – 1969 |
| Contemporary | 1970 – today |

## 2. Century, then decade

1. Scroll the **Century** strip sideways and pick a century, for example *19th c.*. Deep antiquity is grouped into larger blocks such as *3000 BCE* and *2000 BCE*.
2. A row of **decades** appears for that century (1800s, 1810s … 1890s). Pick one to narrow further.
3. Click the selected decade again to go back to the whole century. Click the selected century again to clear the era.

## 3. Custom range

Click **Custom range** to:
- drag the slider (1000 CE to today), or
- type exact years in **From year** and **To year**. Use negative numbers for BCE: −500 means 500 BCE.

## Good to know

- The selected range is shown at the top of the Era section and as a pill above the results.
- Museums often date works as a range ("ca. 1780–1790"). A work appears if its dates **overlap** your range.
- The **Timeline** view (see [Views](views.md)) arranges results along the time axis. It groups them by decade when your range is a century or less.
- Choosing an era also changes the **Style** list: styles from other times are greyed out (see [Exploring by style](exploring-by-style.md)).
