# Artwork details

Click any artwork to open its page.

## What you'll see

- **A large image.** Click it (or the **Zoom** badge) to open a full-screen viewer. Switch between **Fit to screen** and **Actual size**, or click the image to toggle. In Actual size, scroll to move around the image.
- **Title, artist and date.**
- **Style badges.** Click one to explore that style.
- **A description**, when the museum provides one.
- **Details:**
  - artist
  - date
  - place / culture
  - region (a link)
  - century (a link to a timeline)
  - medium
  - credit line
  - licence
- **Related works:** "Also from around …" (the same period and region), "More [style]" and "More from [region]". Each row has a **See all** link that opens Explore with those filters.

## Buttons

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| ♥ | Save to or remove from your favourites |
| View at [museum] | Opens the museum's own page in a new tab, with the full record and reuse terms |
| Copy link | Copies this page's address to share |
| Back | Returns to your results, with your filters intact |

## Licences

- **Public domain / CC0:** you may reuse the image for any purpose.
- **Other:** see the museum's page for terms. V&A and Harvard images, for example, are free for non-commercial use only.
